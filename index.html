03 — Overview

My connection
to Japan

I chose Japan because of its unique landscapes and dynamic geology. The country’s location near several tectonic plate boundaries makes it one of the most geologically active places on Earth. Studying Japan deepened my interest in how natural forces shape both physical geography and human society.

Rivers transport sediment from highlands to the sea, while waves and coastal currents reshape beaches, deltas, and harbors.
